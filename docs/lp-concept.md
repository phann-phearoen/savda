# Savda Landing Page Concept

## The Page's Job

The landing page should answer two questions:

> What is Savda?
>
> What should I discover here?

Savda is not introduced as a shop. It is a trusted place to encounter Cambodian-made products, the people behind them, and the stories that make them worth knowing. The landing page should earn curiosity first; purchase happens later, when a discovery creates intent.

## Page Structure

### 1. Hero

**Purpose:** Establish Savda as a source of unexpected Cambodian discovery.

Lead with a striking, current example of Cambodian-made work rather than a shopping interface. The hero should feel like the cover of an editorial platform: one image, one story, and one clear idea.

**Core message:**

> Discover what Cambodia makes.

Supporting copy explains that Savda finds the products, people, and ideas shaping Cambodia now. The primary action moves directly into the Discovery section: **Explore the stories**.

### 2. Discovery

**Purpose:** Prove that Savda is alive, curious, and actively finding things worth seeing.

This is the main body of the landing page: an image-led editorial contact sheet of products, workshops, food, design, fashion, and makers. It should feel collected rather than catalogued.

Each discovery includes a category, a concise story title, and a path to read more. It never leads with a price, product specification, or purchase action.

The section should make visitors think:

> I did not know this existed.

Not:

> Here is another product listing.

### 3. About Us

**Purpose:** Explain why Savda exists and what its point of view is.

This section combines the former storytelling and "Made in Cambodia, worth knowing" ideas into one concise statement. Savda is a discovery and media layer around Cambodian-made work: it makes good products visible, gives creators context, and helps audiences see craftsmanship, design, culture, and originality where they may not have looked before.

**Core ideas:**

- Good products deserve to be discovered.
- Good creators deserve to be known.
- Cambodian-made can mean design, care, innovation, and originality.

The tone should be confident and observant, not patriotic or promotional. Use a small number of diverse images as evidence, not decoration.

### 4. For Creators

**Purpose:** Invite the people who make the work into the platform.

The message is direct:

> You make it. We tell the story.

Explain that Savda helps Cambodian creators reach interested new audiences through thoughtful storytelling and discovery. Creators retain their own products and fulfillment; Savda contributes visibility, context, and a possible route to purchase when interest is created.

The primary action should be a clear partnership inquiry, such as **Work with Savda**.

### 5. Contact

**Purpose:** Give visitors a simple way to start a relevant conversation.

This is not a generic form block. It is a calm, clear invitation for creators, potential collaborators, press, and people with a discovery to share. A short note defines what contact is for, followed by one direct action: **Contact Savda**.

Contact should be the page's final section, so the visitor leaves with a practical next step after understanding the platform.

## Footer

The footer holds the ongoing relationship rather than competing with the page's primary narrative. It includes:

- Savda mark and a short brand line
- Social links for following new discoveries
- Language switcher
- Copyright and essential legal links

Social links should be present and easy to find, but they do not need their own section or a second large conversion moment. The invitation is simple: follow Savda to keep seeing what Cambodia makes.

## Narrative Flow

```text
LOOK
	↓
DISCOVER
	↓
UNDERSTAND
	↓
CONTRIBUTE
	↓
CONNECT
```

Commerce remains downstream of the landing page. A visitor can shop after encountering a product or story, but the landing page itself should not be structured as a route to a cart.

## Visual Direction

The page should remain:

- Editorial and image-led
- Minimal, warm, and contemporary
- Strongly Khmer in identity without traditional decorative motifs
- More magazine, documentary, and social discovery feed than marketplace
- Anchored by real product photography and the negative-space, continuous-line ស mark

The Hero spends its visual boldness on one featured discovery. The Discovery section carries the page with an asymmetric, editorial image field. About Us, For Creators, and Contact become quieter, more spacious moments that give the imagery and writing room to land.
